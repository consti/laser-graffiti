3 · Calibration
Projector: flashes markers into the corners; drag the yellow corners afterwards to fine-tune. Laser: after a 3 s countdown (shown on the projector), wave the laser around inside the projection for 4 s — thresholds and dot size are learned from it.
4 · Detection
Reflections: the tracker keeps following the blob nearest to the previous position and prefers the brightest one; a new stroke only starts after the dot is seen in 2 consecutive frames.
5 · Drawing
Scales every effect: how much wet ink drips, how many sparks, how big the flames, how hot and how long the burn glows.
Burn: the projector lights the wall softly and your strokes are cut into it as black char with a white-hot → orange → ember edge, smoke and sparks. Scan surface photographs the wall through the camera (projector blanked) and projects that texture back onto itself, so the scorch marks look like they are really in the surface. Needs calibration.
Laser menu: hold the laser in the dashed ☰ circle (in the chosen corner of the projection) to open it; hover an item to select. move menu in the menu cycles the corner.
